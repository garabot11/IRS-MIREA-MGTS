Go2 locomotion policy
Source: https://github.com/fan-ziqi/rl_sar/tree/376d42c9b128f963ab08579762d5a216a976ce39/policy/go2/robot_lab
actor.npz contains the four linear layers of policy.pt. The original uses an identity normalizer and three ELU activations. The NumPy runtime uses the upstream observation order, action scales, gains and joint mapping. License: Apache-2.0 (LICENSE-policy).

SHA256 of original policy.pt: 9f14cb95e74ac9e5e30954da0fc0c33eaa41e337852ebed19fcee869279ade0b
SHA256 of actor.npz: 2c36e2f064de37297a16965dc0323aa24c26ad8f7c8f7753660da809fc33b39c
Reproduce the weight archive from this upstream policy with:
    model = torch.jit.load("policy.pt", map_location="cpu").eval()
    np.savez("actor.npz", **{k.removeprefix("actor."): v.numpy() for k, v in model.state_dict().items()})
